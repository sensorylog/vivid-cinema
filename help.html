Help & FAQ

Getting started

Use Home for recommendations, Discover for filters and search, and My Library for titles you have saved.

How do I find a title?

Use the search field or open Discover and browse by type, genre and other available filters.

Why is a trailer or stream unavailable?

Playback depends on third-party availability, regional restrictions, network conditions and provider changes. Try again later or open another available title.

Account help

If sign-in or account features are not working, check your connection first and then contact us with the page, device and approximate time of the problem.

Still need help?

Visit Contact and send the details needed to reproduce the issue.
