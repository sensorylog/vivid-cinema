Vivid Cinema · Help Centre · Last updated 20 November 2026

Let’s get you back to the good part.

Find practical answers for accounts, playback, your library and the Vivid apps. If you still need help, our support form gives us the details needed to investigate.

Playback is not loading

Playback depends on the selected provider, your network, browser restrictions, regional availability and the provider’s own service. Try refreshing the title, checking your connection or selecting another available source. If the issue persists, contact us with the title, provider, device and approximate time of the failure.

Community features have been retired

The Culture Room, member discussions, Watch Rooms and Cinema Clubs are no longer available. Existing Vivid accounts, personal libraries and individual movie, television, anime and live playback are unaffected. Historical records may remain stored; this product change does not represent a deletion of legacy records.

Vivid Live and external sources

Live channels and embedded playback may be supplied by external providers. Availability and stream quality can change without notice. If one source fails, try another available source or return later. Vivid cannot control the independent uptime, advertising, privacy practices or accessibility of those services.

How does the installed app work?

On supported devices, Vivid can be installed as a progressive web app or used through available Windows and Android builds. The app may cache core application resources for navigation and offline fallback. Live streams, third-party playback and fresh catalogue data generally require an internet connection.

My library changed or disappeared

Some library information may be stored locally while signed out, while account features can synchronise selected information. Clearing browser or app storage, reinstalling the app or changing devices can affect local-only information. Vivid is not a guaranteed backup service.

I cannot sign in

Check your connection, confirm that you are using the correct sign-in method and email address, and retry. If you use Google sign-in, make sure the sign-in window is not blocked. Never send your password or one-time authentication code to support.

How do I delete my account?

Where available, use the Delete Account control in the Account area. The process asks you to reauthenticate before deleting private account data and your Firebase Authentication account. Historical community, room or club records may not be removed automatically by account deletion. Read the Privacy Notice for the current deletion scope and submit a separate privacy request if you need a legacy record reviewed.

Privacy request

Use Contact and choose “Privacy request”. State whether you are requesting access, correction, deletion or objecting to a particular use. We may verify your identity before responding. Do not send passwords, one-time codes or identity documents through the ordinary contact form.

Copyright or rights complaint

Use the Contact page and choose “Copyright or rights complaint”. Identify the material and its location, explain the right you own or are authorised to represent, and provide a reliable reply address.

Security vulnerability

Use Contact and choose “Security vulnerability report”. Share safe reproduction steps and the affected page. Do not exploit the issue, access another person’s information, disrupt the service or publish sensitive details before giving us a reasonable opportunity to investigate.

Still need help?

Visit Vivid Contact. For privacy and data practices, read the Privacy Notice; for service rules, read the Terms of Use.
