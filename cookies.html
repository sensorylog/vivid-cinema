Vivid Cinema · Cookie & Storage Policy · Last updated 20 November 2026

Technology that remembers what matters.

This Policy explains how Vivid Cinema uses cookies, browser storage and similar technologies to keep the service working, remember your choices and understand product performance.

1. Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small data file stored by a website. Vivid and its providers may also use localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, service-worker caches and similar browser technologies. These technologies have different lifetimes and purposes; this Policy describes them together where appropriate.

2. Essential application storage

Some storage supports core functions such as sign-in persistence, security checks, navigation, PWA installation and offline application resources. If you block or clear this storage, you may be signed out, asked to verify again or lose access to features that depend on saved application state.

3. Preferences and local features

Vivid may store language and display preferences, reduced-motion settings, provider-country choices, autoplay preferences, local library information, recent searches and navigation state on your device. Local information may not be synchronised to your account and may be lost if you clear browser data, reinstall the app or change devices.

4. Session measurement for signed-in members

Vivid’s current first-party product measurement is designed for signed-in members. It uses a session identifier stored in sessionStorage and records selected product events in Vivid’s analytics data store, such as page views, session starts, content interactions, sign-in/sign-up events, download clicks and native-app first opens. Search measurement records query length rather than raw search text. The current implementation does not create anonymous Firebase accounts or write Vivid analytics events merely because a public visitor opens a page.

Session storage is normally cleared when the browser tab/session ends, subject to browser behaviour. Analytics records stored on the service side are governed by the Privacy Notice and its retention provisions.

5. Third-party embeds and providers

When you load a YouTube trailer, sign in through Google/Firebase, or open a third-party playback or live-channel provider, that service may set or read its own cookies or similar identifiers and receive technical information directly from your browser. Vivid does not control the independent technologies or retention practices of those providers. Their own policies apply.

6. Advertising technologies

The current Vivid website build does not intentionally load the previously evaluated Adcash Autotag as part of its first-party application shell. This does not prevent an independent third-party player or embedded provider from displaying its own advertising or using its own technologies. If Vivid introduces a first-party advertising or measurement provider in the future, this Policy will be updated to describe it before or when the relevant technology is enabled, as required by law.

7. Your controls

  • Use your browser settings to view, block or delete cookies and site storage.
  • Use your device or browser controls to clear Vivid’s local and session data.
  • Use available account settings to change preferences stored by Vivid.
  • Choose whether to load a third-party embed or playback provider; once loaded, its own technologies may apply.

Blocking all storage may affect authentication, saved preferences, offline behaviour and other essential features. Browser controls do not necessarily remove information already held by a third-party provider or by Vivid’s service-side systems.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when browser technologies, providers or product features change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.

9. Contact

For questions about cookies, local storage or tracking, use Vivid Contact and select “Privacy request”. For broader information about personal data, read the Privacy Notice.
