Cookie Policy

Vivid uses cookies, local storage and similar technologies selectively to make the product work, remember preferences and support requested features.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files stored by a website. Similar browser technologies include localStorage, IndexedDB, service-worker caches and other client-side storage. This Policy uses “cookies” as a general term where appropriate.

2. Essential technologies

Some storage is necessary for core functions such as authentication state, security, preferences, PWA operation and local library functionality. Disabling or clearing required storage can sign you out, reset preferences or reduce functionality.

3. Preference and functionality storage

Vivid may store interface preferences, language choices, library state or application settings locally so the experience remains consistent between visits. These values are not a substitute for a secure account database.

4. Analytics and measurement

Where analytics services are enabled, they may receive technical and usage information needed to understand performance and product usage. Analytics configuration may change as the product evolves. We aim to use measurement for product and reliability purposes rather than collecting unrelated personal information.

5. Third-party technologies

Embedded trailers, authentication services, payment services, playback providers and other third-party integrations may set or access their own cookies or similar technologies. Those providers are responsible for their own technology and privacy practices.

6. Managing storage

Your browser provides controls for viewing, blocking and deleting cookies and site storage. You can also clear Vivid's stored application data through your browser or device settings. Some Vivid features may stop working correctly after required storage is removed.

7. Updates

We may update this Policy when technologies or providers change. The effective date at the top will be updated with a new version.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy or tracking can be sent through Contact. For the broader data practices, see our Privacy Notice.
