The Vivid story · Last updated 20 November 2026

Made for the love of cinema.

Vivid Cinema brings discovery, useful film and television information, personal collections and shared viewing into one cinematic experience — built to feel considered on every screen.

A focused entertainment experience

Vivid brings together movie and television discovery, catalogue details, recommendations, trailers, provider-availability information, personal library tools, live-channel discovery and third-party playback options. The experience is designed for phones, tablets, desktop computers and supported installed apps.

Watch together

Watch Rooms let people gather around a shared screening. Hosts can invite members, manage a shared queue and choose room permissions. Where a verified player integration supports remote commands and playback events, Vivid can coordinate play, pause and seeking. Unsupported providers remain available only for individual playback and are not presented as synchronised sources.

Cinema Clubs and other community features may provide shared lists, discussion and member activity. Information contributed to shared spaces can be visible to other members; use those spaces thoughtfully.

Our principles

  • Clarity: make it clear what a feature does and when an external provider is involved.
  • Reliability: keep core discovery and navigation useful when a third-party integration is unavailable.
  • Privacy: use information for defined product, security and legal purposes, and explain the relevant choices.
  • Craft: build a consistent, accessible interface across supported devices.
  • Responsible integrations: distinguish Vivid’s own interface and software from independent catalogue, media and playback services.

Catalogue and media sources

Vivid may use TMDB for movie and television metadata and provider-availability information, YouTube for trailers, Firebase/Google for selected application infrastructure, and other providers for playback and live-channel functionality. Information supplied by external sources may change, be delayed or be unavailable in a particular territory.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. TMDB attribution guidelines.

Streaming availability information is provided by JustWatch via TMDB where available. Availability can vary by country and change over time.

Vivid does not claim ownership of third-party films, series, music, artwork, trailers, channel feeds, trademarks, metadata or independent provider services because they are referenced or displayed in the product. Rights remain with their respective owners. Vivid does not guarantee that an external source is available, licensed in every territory or continuously accessible.
