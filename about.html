Vivid Cinema · About

Built for people who take watching seriously.

Vivid Cinema is a cinematic discovery and viewing interface built to make movies, television and live channels easier to discover, understand and access.

What Vivid does

Vivid brings discovery, title information, recommendations, trailers, availability information, personal library features and live television into one focused experience. We design the product around clarity: useful information first, fewer unnecessary steps, and a consistent experience across phone, tablet, desktop and installed PWA.

What Vivid does not claim

Vivid does not claim ownership of third-party films, series, artwork, trailers, channel feeds, metadata or provider services merely because they appear through the product. Rights belong to their respective owners and providers. Availability can vary by territory, rights window, device, network and provider.

Our product principles

  • Clarity: users should understand what a feature does and where information comes from.
  • Reliability: core navigation should remain useful even when a third-party service is unavailable.
  • Privacy: collect and retain information for defined product, security or legal purposes rather than because it is available.
  • Accessibility: interfaces should work across screen sizes, input methods and reasonable accessibility settings.
  • Responsible integrations: third-party services are identified as third-party services and are not represented as Vivid-owned technology.

Catalogue and data sources

Vivid may use services including TMDB for movie and TV metadata and provider-availability information, YouTube for trailers, Firebase/Google infrastructure for selected application functions, and other providers for specific playback or live-channel functionality. Provider information can change without notice.

Vivid uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Where availability data is supplied through TMDB's JustWatch-powered provider data, the applicable JustWatch attribution remains with that data.

Accounts and your library

Some features can work without an account, while account features can provide synchronisation and personalised functionality. Certain preferences and library information may also be stored locally in your browser. Local storage can be cleared by your device or browser.

Security and responsible reporting

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to a web application and take credible security reports seriously. If you discover a vulnerability, report it through Contact rather than exploiting it or accessing another person's information.

Our legal framework

Vivid publishes separate Terms of Use, Privacy Notice, Cookie Policy and Accessibility Statement. Where Ghanaian law applies, our privacy framework is intended to reflect the principles of the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). This website is not a substitute for legal advice or a formal compliance assessment.

Operator information

Vivid Cinema is the public product name. The operator's formal legal entity, registered address and data-protection registration details should be published here once those details are finalised. We will not invent corporate information simply to make the page look more established.

Stay informed

Material changes to the service or policies are reflected in the applicable policy's effective date. For questions, rights requests, accessibility issues or security reports, use Contact Vivid Cinema.
