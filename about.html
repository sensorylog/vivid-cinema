VIVID CINEMA

About Vivid Cinema

Vivid Cinema is a movie and TV discovery experience built to make finding something to watch feel simple, cinematic and fast.

What Vivid does

Browse films and series, explore trending titles, open detailed title pages, manage a personal library and use supported playback links where available.

Our approach

Vivid Cinema focuses on a calm interface, useful discovery tools, responsive design and clear information. Availability, ratings, artwork and title metadata may come from third-party services and can change over time.

Third-party services

Vivid Cinema may use external providers for movie data, images, authentication and playback. Their own terms and privacy policies apply to their services.
