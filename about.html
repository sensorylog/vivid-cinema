Vivid Cinema · About

Movies should be easier to find.

Vivid Cinema is a cinematic discovery service designed to help you find movies and series quickly, understand what they are, and move from discovery to an available viewing option with as little friction as possible.

What Vivid is

Vivid brings together movie and TV discovery in one focused interface: trending titles, search, genres, streaming-service filters, title details, trailers, recommendations and personal library features. The goal is not to overwhelm you with a catalogue. It is to help you decide what to watch.

How the catalogue works

Vivid does not create or claim ownership of the movie and TV metadata, posters, ratings, trailers or availability information displayed in the app. These elements can be supplied by third-party services such as TMDB and YouTube and can change as those services update their catalogues. Streaming availability is particularly dependent on country and provider.

Where Vivid sends you to an external viewing or service provider, that provider operates its own service under its own terms and privacy practices. Vivid does not represent that a third-party title, stream or service will always be available.

Your account and library

An account can make selected features more useful, including synchronised library information and preferences. Some information can also remain locally in your browser. We aim to collect only what the feature needs and to explain the important uses of personal data in our Privacy Notice.

Privacy by design

Vivid is built around data minimisation, purpose limitation, security and user control. We do not need your private messages, payment-card details or unrelated personal information to provide movie discovery. If you contact us, please share only the information necessary to resolve your request.

Security and responsible operation

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the service, including authenticated access controls, secure transport and security-focused hosting configuration. No online service can promise absolute security. Suspected security or privacy issues should be reported through Contact so they can be investigated.

Ghana and your data

Where Ghana's data-protection law applies, Vivid's privacy practices are intended to reflect the principles and data-subject rights established by the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). Those include transparency, access, correction, objection and other rights described in our Privacy Notice. The Data Protection Commission is Ghana's statutory data-protection regulator.

This page describes Vivid's product and privacy approach; it is not a certification of legal compliance or legal advice. The operator should complete any required registration, appointments, assessments and organisational controls before relying on the site as legally compliant.

Third-party services

Vivid may rely on Firebase/Google services for authentication and hosting, TMDB for catalogue information, YouTube for trailers, and other providers for specific features. Each provider remains responsible for processing it performs on its own systems. See our Privacy Notice for more detail.

Our standard

Vivid should feel premium on the surface and disciplined underneath: fast navigation, clear information, restrained data collection, accessible controls and honest boundaries about what comes from Vivid and what comes from another service.
