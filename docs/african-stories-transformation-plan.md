# African Stories — seven-phase transformation programme

African Stories is a major planned destination for African feature films, documentaries, historical storytelling, archival moving images, and the people and contexts behind them. It is not a social network or a small genre rail.

Every title must have an explicit access state: **Stream on Vivid** (rights and technical permission confirmed), **Watch officially** (verified external viewing route), **Discover / archive record** (catalogued, no current public viewing route confirmed), or **Access pending** (source/rights relationship under investigation). Metadata or archive possession does not grant streaming rights.

## Seven phases — one PR

### Phase 1 — Retire community
Retire Culture Room, community feed, polls, member/topic follows, article discussions, Watch Rooms and Cinema Clubs from the active interface. Keep newsroom publishing and sourced story reading. Preserve Firestore records, rules, indexes, server/API routes and dormant modules; no bulk deletion or destructive migration.

### Phase 2 — Deep African content ecosystem research
Map film archives, cinematheques, national libraries, broadcasters, universities, museums and preservation projects; documentary platforms, distributors, festivals and filmmaker networks; metadata sources, identifiers, APIs and commercial restrictions; authorized playback providers, embeds, streaming APIs and territorial availability; rights holders, licensing windows, territory, language, subtitle, monetization, restoration and takedown requirements; competitors; regional/country/language gaps across West, East, North, Central and Southern Africa and the diaspora. Record source URL, organization, geography, content type, evidence date, access route, rights position, playback status, commercial constraints, partnership path, confidence and open questions for every lead.

### Phase 3 — Product and data blueprint
Define taxonomy, title/edition identity, alternate titles, country/region, language, format, genre, historical period, subject, people/organizations, release/restoration metadata, synopsis, provenance, references, editorial review, rights holder, license scope, territory, expiry, provider source, access state and playback verification. Design country hubs, collections, timelines, people pages, filmographies and contextual links. Distinguish documentary, oral history, newsreel, archive footage and fictional historical drama.

### Phase 4 — Dedicated African player
Create a third playback system isolated from existing movie/TV and anime players. Include provider adapters, title identity resolution, authorized source records, source health, rights/territory gating, safe embeds, authorized HLS/DASH, telemetry, fallback and unavailable states. Never scrape or bypass access controls.

### Phase 5 — African Stories experience
Build a premium destination integrated with Vivid's existing shell: country/region pages, collections, people, timelines, historical context, filmographies, linked discovery and language filters. Optimize mobile, low bandwidth, accessibility and existing device support.

### Phase 6 — Acquisition and partnerships
Establish a rights-aware acquisition pipeline and contact strategy for filmmakers, archives, national institutions, distributors, festivals and authorized platforms. Agreements and title-level rights records precede playback. Restoration/digitization partnerships require clear rights, custody and technical responsibilities.

### Phase 7 — QA, staged launch and continental expansion
Run catalogue audits, title-level rights checks, provider playback checks, metadata QA, editorial fact checks, security, accessibility, performance and device tests. Launch with a verified collection and expand in waves. Measure playable coverage separately from catalogue size.

## Preservation constraints
No rebuild. Preserve Firebase Authentication, Firestore user data, personal library, movie/TV/anime/live playback, downloads, SEO, PWA and Android/Desktop/Web support. No unrelated provider/data-model changes without documented need. No destructive deletion of community data during Phase 1. No unverified claims of rights, availability, partnership or API capability. All seven phases remain in one feature branch and one PR; Phase 2 starts only after Phase 1 exit criteria are met.
