A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you go.

Do I need to contribute to use Vivid?

No. Vivid remains free to enjoy. Contributions are voluntary and do not unlock features or change how you are treated.

Where does my contribution go?

Contributions support the ongoing costs and development of Vivid, including reliability, discovery, playback and improvements across devices. We do not promise that a specific payment will fund a particular feature.

Can I contribute from outside Ghana?

Yes. Eligible international Visa and Mastercard holders can use Paystack. Paystack prices this checkout in GHS, and your card issuer may convert the amount to your local currency or apply its own fees. Gumroad remains available as an alternative international checkout.

How does supporter recognition work?

Recognition is optional. Submit the form above after contributing. We verify the contribution and only publish the display name you explicitly approved.
