Accessibility

Vivid Cinema is designed to be usable across desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Built-in considerations

  • Keyboard-focusable controls and links.
  • Responsive layouts that adapt to smaller screens.
  • Reduced-motion support where animations are not essential.
  • Readable contrast and scalable text.
  • Labels for important interactive controls.

Need an accommodation?

If you encounter a barrier, please contact us and describe what you were trying to do and the device or browser you were using.
