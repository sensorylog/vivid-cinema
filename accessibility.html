Vivid Cinema · Accessibility · Last updated 20 November 2026

Cinema should be open to everyone.

We want Vivid Cinema to be understandable and usable across different devices, abilities and ways of interacting. Accessibility is an ongoing product responsibility, not a one-time checklist.

Our commitment

We aim to improve Vivid in line with recognised accessibility practices, using the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA as a design reference where reasonably applicable. This is an improvement target, not a claim that every page or feature has been independently audited or certified as conformant.

Current design considerations

  • Semantic page structure and meaningful headings.
  • Keyboard access and visible focus indicators for supported interactive controls.
  • Responsive layouts and scalable text across phone, tablet and desktop screens.
  • Readable contrast and clear labels for important controls.
  • Status messages for actions and errors where supported.
  • Reduced-motion preferences for non-essential animation where implemented.
  • Playback controls and navigation designed to remain usable across supported devices.

Known limitations

Some experiences depend on independent third parties, including embedded video players, trailers, live streams, authentication interfaces and external catalogue services. Their accessibility, keyboard behaviour, captions, screen-reader support and visual presentation may be controlled by those providers rather than Vivid. Some advanced or newly introduced features may also have accessibility gaps that we have not yet identified.

How to report an accessibility barrier

If a barrier prevents you from completing a task, please use Contact and choose “Accessibility feedback”. Tell us:

  • the page or feature you were using;
  • what you were trying to do and what happened;
  • your device, operating system and browser or app version, if known;
  • the assistive technology or input method involved, if you are comfortable sharing it; and
  • what change would make the experience usable.

Please do not send medical records or other sensitive personal information. We may ask follow-up questions to understand and reproduce the issue.

Review and improvement

We review accessibility feedback as part of product maintenance and may update this Statement as the interface, providers and applicable standards evolve. We do not currently publish a third-party accessibility certification or guarantee that all third-party media is accessible.

Contact

For accessibility assistance or to request an alternative way to complete an important task, use Vivid Contact.
