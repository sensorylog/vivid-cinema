Privacy

A plain-language view of what information Vivid Cinema uses, where it is stored and what happens when third-party services are involved.

Information you provide

When you create an account, Firebase Authentication processes information needed to authenticate you, such as your email address and authentication identifier. Your display name may also be stored. If you contact us, the name, email address, message and any optional screenshot you submit are processed by the contact service used by the site.

Library and preferences

Favorites, Watch Later items and history can be stored in your browser. When you are signed in, selected library items and account preferences may also be synchronized to your account data.

Catalogue and search requests

Vivid Cinema connects your browser to services that provide movie and TV catalogue information, artwork, search results, trailers and availability information. These requests are necessary for discovery features to work.

Playback

When you start playback or load a trailer, content may be embedded from a third-party provider. That provider may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser information and the requested resource under its own privacy policy. Vivid Cinema does not proxy third-party media through its own server.

Local storage

The app uses browser storage for local library data and preferences. Clearing site data can remove information that exists only on your device.

Security

Authenticated account data uses Firebase Authentication and owner-scoped account access rules. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your credentials private and report suspected security issues through the Contact page.

Your choices

You can sign out and clear local browser data through the app or your browser settings. You can also contact us about account-data requests. We may need enough information to identify the relevant account and verify a request before acting on it.

Third-party policies

Firebase, Google, TMDB, YouTube, playback providers and the contact service have their own privacy practices. Their policies govern information they process when their services are used.
