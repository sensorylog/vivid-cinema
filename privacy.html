Privacy

A plain-language summary of information used to operate Vivid Cinema.

Information you provide

When you create an account, Firebase Authentication processes information needed to authenticate you, such as your email address and authentication identifier. Your display name may also be stored. If you contact us, the name, email address, message and optional screenshot you submit are processed by the contact service used by the site.

Library and preferences

Favorites, Watch Later items and history can be stored in your browser. When signed in, these library items and selected account preferences may be synchronized to your owner-scoped Firestore account data.

Service requests

Vivid Cinema requests movie and TV catalogue information, artwork, search results, trailers and availability information from third-party services such as TMDB and YouTube. Your browser also connects to Firebase services for authentication and account data.

Playback

When you start playback, the selected title may load inside a third-party embedded player. That provider may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser information and the requested resource according to its own policies. Vivid Cinema does not proxy the media through its own server.

Local storage

The app uses browser storage for local library data and preferences. Clearing site data can remove information stored only on your device.

Security

Authenticated account data is protected by Firebase Authentication and owner-scoped Firestore security rules. No web application can guarantee absolute security, so keep credentials private and report suspected security issues through the contact channel.

Your choices and requests

You can sign out and clear local browser data through your browser settings. You may request account-data assistance through the Contact page. We may need enough information to identify the relevant account and may verify the request before taking action.

Third-party policies

Firebase, Google, TMDB, YouTube, playback providers and the contact service have their own privacy practices. Review those policies when their services are used.
