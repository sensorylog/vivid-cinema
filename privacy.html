Privacy

A clear summary of the information Vivid Cinema uses to provide the service.

Account information

If you create an account, Firebase Authentication may process your email address, authentication identifiers and account credentials. Your display name and viewing preferences may also be stored with your account.

Library information

Favorites, Watch Later items and viewing history can be stored locally on your device. When you are signed in, Vivid Cinema can synchronize these library items to your owner-scoped Firestore account data.

Movie and TV requests

Vivid Cinema requests catalogue metadata, artwork and availability information from third-party services such as TMDB. These requests are needed to operate search, discovery and title pages.

Third-party playback

When you start playback, the selected title may be loaded in an embedded third-party player. That provider may process information according to its own policies. Vivid Cinema does not download or proxy the media through its own server.

Contact

If you contact Vivid Cinema, information you include in your message is processed through the contact service used by the site. Avoid sending passwords, payment details or other sensitive information through the contact form.

Your choices

You can stop using the service, sign out, and remove local browser data through your browser settings. Account-related deletion requests can be made through the available contact channel.
