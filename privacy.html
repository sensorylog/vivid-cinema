Vivid Cinema · Privacy Notice · Last updated 20 November 2026

Privacy, handled with care.

This Privacy Notice explains how Vivid Cinema collects, uses, stores and shares personal information when you browse, create an account, use your library, join a Watch Room or contact us. It also explains the choices and rights available to you.

1. Who this Notice covers

This Notice applies to the Vivid Cinema website, installed progressive web app (PWA), Windows and Android applications, accounts, personal library features, Watch Rooms, Cinema Clubs and related support services operated under the Vivid Cinema name.

Data Protection Commission registration status (21 November 2026): Vivid Cinema has not yet been registered with Ghana’s Data Protection Commission, and no registration number is claimed. Section 27 of Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) requires a data controller to register before processing personal data. Publishing this Notice does not replace that requirement, create an exemption or establish that Vivid is registered. Registration remains outstanding and must be addressed with the Commission and qualified Ghanaian privacy counsel.

2. Who is responsible for your information

Vivid Cinema is the product name. The creator publicly identified for the product is Johnson Kyei Baffour; creator attribution does not, by itself, establish the legal identity of the data controller. The full legal name and service or postal address of the person or entity acting as data controller have not yet been supplied for publication. Those details remain necessary for a complete controller disclosure. We will not invent a company name, registered address, registration number or Data Protection Commission certificate.

For privacy questions and rights requests, use the Vivid Contact form and select “Privacy request”. Do not include passwords or verification codes.

3. Information we process

  • Account and profile data: Firebase user identifier, email address, display name, profile image reference, sign-in provider, verification state and account preferences.
  • Library and viewing data: favourites, watch-later items, viewing history, progress, completion status, saved titles, taste preferences and related synchronisation records, depending on the features you use.
  • Watch Room and Cinema Club data: room or club names, invitation references, member identifiers, display names, roles, membership and presence status, queue items, votes, chat messages, reactions, moderation or membership actions, selected player/server, playback position and shared playback state. Other members of the relevant room or club can see information made available within that shared space.
  • Product analytics for signed-in members: page views, session starts, content interactions, stream-button interactions, account sign-in or sign-up events, download-link clicks and native-app first-open events. These records may include your account identifier, a session identifier, platform, timestamp, page path, content ID/type and limited event details.
  • Search information: Search terms are sent to TMDB when needed to retrieve catalogue results. Recent search history may be stored locally in your browser and is not synced to Vivid account data. The raw search text is not uploaded as behaviour-event metadata; the current product analytics implementation records search activity using query length rather than uploading the search text.
  • Device and technical information: browser or app type, operating system/platform, approximate network and diagnostic information, security signals, request metadata and local storage state processed by the relevant infrastructure or provider.
  • Support and legal-request information: name, reply email, request category, message, page or room reference and any optional screenshot you choose to submit through Contact.
  • Push and reminder information: where you enable these features, notification subscription endpoint and key material, device/browser user-agent information, reminder settings and related delivery state.

4. Analytics: what is and is not collected

Vivid’s first-party product analytics are designed to operate for signed-in Vivid members. The current implementation does not create an anonymous Firebase account or write Vivid analytics events merely because a public visitor opens a page. When a signed-in member uses the product, Vivid may record the event categories described above in its analytics data store.

Analytics event details are filtered to avoid sending direct contact details, passwords, authentication tokens, message text, raw search text or precise location as event metadata. This filtering is a safeguard, not a promise that infrastructure providers never receive technical request information in the ordinary course of delivering their services.

5. Why we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; provide sign-in and account recovery; operate libraries and preferences; return search and catalogue results; create and maintain Watch Rooms and Cinema Clubs; show room membership and presence; synchronise supported playback; provide support; understand product reliability and usage; prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents; enforce our Terms; and comply with legal obligations.

6. Legal grounds and fairness

Where Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) applies, Vivid must process personal data lawfully, fairly and for specified purposes, and must not collect more than is necessary. Depending on the purpose and circumstances, processing may be based on consent, steps needed to provide a requested service, a legal obligation, protection of a person’s interests, or another ground permitted by applicable law. Where consent is the relevant ground, it may be withdrawn subject to applicable law and the consequences explained when consent is requested.

7. When information is shared

We do not sell personal information as a business model. Information may be processed or disclosed to service providers that support features you use, to other users within a shared Watch Room or Cinema Club, to protect the service and its users, or where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

8. Service providers and external services

  • Google Firebase: authentication, database and related application infrastructure.
  • Cloudflare: website delivery, security and server-side application routes.
  • Vercel: certain legacy or separately deployed API functions, including the verification-email route where it is used.
  • TMDB: movie and television catalogue information, search and provider-availability information. Search queries needed to return results are sent to TMDB.
  • YouTube: trailer playback when a YouTube embed is selected.
  • Formspree: contact-form submission handling, including the message and any optional screenshot you attach.
  • Third-party playback and live-channel providers: when you select or load an external player or live source, that provider may receive your IP address, browser/device details, page or embed URL, playback requests and other technical information directly from your device. The provider controls its own systems and privacy practices.

Provider names and integrations can change as the product evolves. A provider’s processing is also governed by its own privacy notice and terms. Review those notices before using an external service if you need to understand its independent practices.

9. Shared Watch Rooms and Clubs

Watch Rooms are designed for shared viewing. Room hosts and members may see participant display names, roles, presence, queue suggestions, votes, chat and reactions, and the player/server or playback state shared with the room. Do not put private, confidential or sensitive information into a room chat or queue title. Invitation links and codes should be shared only with people you intend to invite.

Room and club records are stored to operate the shared feature and protect its integrity. Expiry of an invitation or room does not necessarily mean every related record is immediately erased from backups, logs or retained operational data.

10. International processing

Our infrastructure and service providers may process information in countries outside Ghana or your country of residence. Applicable transfer requirements and safeguards must be assessed for the relevant provider and processing activity. Provider-specific details may be updated as our infrastructure changes.

11. Retention

We keep information for no longer than reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to security, dispute-resolution, legal and operational requirements. Different records have different lifecycles. Local browser data may remain until you clear it, your browser removes it or Vivid overwrites it. Shared room and club content may remain in shared records even after you stop participating.

12. Account deletion

You can request permanent account deletion from the Account area, where available. The current self-service process reauthenticates you, deletes the account profile and the listed private library, preference and account subcollections, clears Vivid-prefixed local and session storage on that browser, and then deletes the Firebase Authentication account.

Important limitation: the current self-service deletion routine does not automatically remove every contribution from shared Watch Room or Cinema Club records, or erase copies held by third-party providers, security logs or backups. A display name, message, membership or other shared contribution may therefore remain. If you need a shared contribution reviewed for removal or de-identification, submit a separate Privacy request. We will assess it under applicable law and the rights of other participants.

13. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law and its exceptions, you may have rights to be informed about processing, request access to your information, request correction of inaccurate information, object to certain processing, and seek deletion or other remedies. The rights available and the time and manner for responding depend on the law that applies to your request.

Submit a request through Contact and choose “Privacy request”. Tell us the right you wish to exercise and provide enough information for us to locate the relevant account or record. We may ask for reasonable identity verification. We will never ask for your password or one-time code.

If you are in Ghana, you may also contact the Ghana Data Protection Commission for information about data-subject rights and its complaint process.

14. Security

We use technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the service, including encrypted transport, authentication, access controls, database rules and security-focused hosting configuration. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account or information has been compromised, contact us promptly and change your account credentials through the relevant provider.

15. Children and age restrictions

Vivid is not designed to encourage children to disclose personal information. You must meet the age and legal-capacity requirements that apply to your use of the service. Where a parent or guardian’s consent or supervision is required by law, it must be obtained before a child uses the relevant feature. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so we can review the matter.

16. Cookies and local storage

Vivid uses browser storage for authentication state, application preferences, local library functions, recent searches, navigation and PWA operation. Signed-in product analytics also use a session identifier held in session storage. Third-party embeds may set their own cookies or similar technologies. See the Cookie Policy for details.

17. Changes to this Notice

We may update this Notice when our services, providers, data practices or legal obligations change. The updated date will appear at the top of the page. Material changes will be highlighted where appropriate.

18. Contact and complaints

For privacy questions, access or correction requests, deletion requests, objections or complaints, use Vivid Contact and select “Privacy request”. Please share only the information needed to identify and resolve your request.
