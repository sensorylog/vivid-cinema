Privacy Notice

We want you to know what information Vivid uses, why it uses it, where third parties are involved, how long information is kept, and how you can exercise your rights.

1. Who this notice is for

This notice applies to personal information processed through Vivid Cinema's website and app features. It is written with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) in mind where that law applies. It does not replace a formal legal assessment of the operator's obligations, registration status or processing activities.

2. Data controller

For Vivid-operated processing, the Vivid Cinema operator is the party responsible for deciding why and how the information is processed. The service currently uses the product name “Vivid Cinema” publicly; the operator's full legal identity and formal data-protection registration details should be published here once the operating entity and registration are finalised. This avoids inventing legal details that have not been supplied.

3. Information we may process

  • Account data: email address, authentication identifier and, where supplied, display name or profile information.
  • Library data: favourites, Watch Later items, viewing/history information and selected preferences when the feature is used.
  • Device and technical information: browser, device, network and security information that may be processed by hosting, authentication, analytics or embedded third-party services.
  • Support information: name, email, reason for contact, message and optional screenshot submitted through the Contact page.
  • Catalogue activity: searches, title requests and filter selections needed to provide discovery features. Catalogue requests may be sent to third-party providers.

4. Information we do not ask you to send

Do not submit passwords, authentication codes, payment-card numbers, private keys, government ID numbers or unrelated sensitive information through the contact form. Vivid does not need that information for ordinary movie discovery.

5. Why information is processed

Depending on the feature, information is processed to create and secure accounts, provide library functions, respond to support requests, deliver catalogue and search functionality, maintain service security, diagnose failures and comply with legal obligations. We aim to use personal information only for specified and relevant purposes and to avoid collecting information that is unnecessary for the feature.

6. Lawful basis and consent

Where Ghanaian law applies, processing should have an appropriate lawful basis under Act 843. Depending on the activity, this may include consent, performance of a service or contract, compliance with a legal obligation, protection of rights or another basis recognised by law. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent, although doing so can prevent a feature from working.

7. Ghana data-subject rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, data subjects have rights that include being informed about processing, access to personal data, correction of inaccurate or incomplete information, objection to certain processing, prevention of processing in specified circumstances and remedies provided by law. The Data Protection Commission identifies these rights under sections 39–44 of Act 843.

To exercise a Vivid privacy right, use Contact and choose Privacy request. We may ask for reasonable information needed to verify that the request concerns the relevant account. We will not ask for your password.

8. Access and correction requests

When a valid request is received, we will review it and respond according to applicable law. The Ghana DPC states that an access request under the Act should be complied with promptly and, subject to the Act, within forty days. Some requests can be limited by law where another person's rights, security, legal obligations or other recognised exceptions apply.

9. Retention

We aim not to keep personal data longer than necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legal obligations, security, dispute handling or legitimate operational needs. Exact retention periods can differ by data category and third-party provider. Local browser data can remain until you clear it or the browser removes it; account data may remain while the account or a legitimate retention need exists.

10. Sharing and recipients

Information may be processed by service providers that help Vivid operate, such as authentication, hosting, database, form-processing, catalogue, image, trailer or playback providers. We do not sell personal information as a business model. We may disclose information where necessary to provide a requested service, protect users or the service, investigate abuse, comply with lawful requests, or satisfy legal obligations.

11. Third-party services

Vivid may use Firebase/Google services for hosting, authentication and related infrastructure; TMDB for catalogue and availability information; YouTube for trailers; and Formspree for the Contact form. Those providers can process information on their own systems under their own privacy terms. When you open an external provider or embedded playback experience, that provider may receive technical information directly from your browser.

12. International processing

Some service providers may process information outside Ghana. Where personal data is transferred or made accessible across borders, the operator should assess the applicable requirements of Act 843 and the safeguards required for the particular transfer and processor relationship. Vivid does not claim that every third-party provider is a Vivid data processor; the relationship must be assessed provider by provider.

13. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the service, including HTTPS, access controls, authenticated account boundaries and security-focused hosting configuration. No system connected to the internet is completely secure. We maintain procedures for investigating credible security incidents and will take steps required by applicable law when an incident affects personal data.

14. Cookies and local storage

Vivid uses browser storage and similar client-side mechanisms for functions such as preferences, local library information and application state. Third-party services may use their own cookies or similar technologies when their content is loaded. Browser controls can limit or remove locally stored information, but doing so can affect functionality.

15. Children's privacy

Vivid is not designed as a service for children who are too young to lawfully consent to personal-data processing. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us through the Privacy request route so we can review the situation.

16. Security and privacy complaints

Start with the Contact page so we can investigate. If you are in Ghana and believe your data-protection rights have been infringed, the Data Protection Commission provides complaint and breach-reporting channels. The DPC is Ghana's statutory regulator under Act 843.

17. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our processing, features or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top will be updated when a new version is published. Material changes will be described clearly where appropriate.

18. Questions

For privacy requests, data-access or correction requests, objection notices, deletion questions or security concerns, use Contact us. Please include enough information to identify the issue but never include your password or authentication code.
