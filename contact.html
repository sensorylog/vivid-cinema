Vivid Cinema · Customer Care · Last updated 20 November 2026

We’re listening.

Whether something is not working, you need help with your account, or you want to exercise a privacy right, send us a clear note. We’ll route it to the right support category.

Your information deserves care.

Share only what is needed to investigate. Never send a password, one-time verification code, private key, full payment-card number or government identity document through this form.

Product support

Sign-in, account settings, search, library, downloads, playback and general product issues.

Watch Rooms

Invitations, room membership, shared queues, chat, host permissions and playback synchronisation.

Privacy & trust

Access, correction, deletion, objections, accessibility feedback, copyright notices and responsible security reports.

This form is submitted to Formspree, a third-party form service used by Vivid. Information you submit is used to review and respond to your request. Read the Privacy Notice before submitting.

For a faster investigation

  • Technical issue: include the page or title, device, browser/app version, approximate time and the steps that led to the issue.
  • Watch Room issue: include the room reference ID, which player/server was selected and whether the issue affected the host, a guest or both. Do not send the invite secret.
  • Privacy request: state whether you are requesting access, correction, deletion or objection. We may ask for proportionate identity verification before acting.
  • Copyright or rights complaint: identify the material and its location, explain the right you own or represent, and provide a reliable reply address.
  • Security report: describe the affected page, impact and safe reproduction steps. Do not access another person’s data, disrupt the service or publicly disclose an unremediated vulnerability.

Response expectations

We review genuine requests and aim to respond as promptly as reasonably possible. Response times vary with the nature of the issue, verification requirements and the information available. This form is not an emergency service and submission does not create a guaranteed response deadline.
