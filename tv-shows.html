Where can I watch TV shows for free?

Streaming rights vary by series, country and provider. Vivid helps you discover shows and understand their details; check the viewing provider for current free-with-ads, subscription or rental availability. Browsing Vivid itself is free, but that does not make every listed series free to stream.
