Terms of Use

A clear guide to using Vivid Cinema, including accounts, catalogue information and third-party playback.

1. Using Vivid Cinema

Vivid Cinema is a movie and TV discovery service. It presents catalogue information, artwork, trailers, availability information and, where enabled, embedded playback experiences supplied by third-party services.

2. Third-party services

Movie and TV metadata, artwork, trailers, availability and playback may be supplied by third parties. Those services can change or become unavailable, and their own terms and policies also apply when you use them through Vivid Cinema.

3. Accounts

If you create an account, provide accurate information and keep your sign-in credentials private. You are responsible for activity performed through your account. Email verification may be required for email-and-password accounts.

4. Your library

Favorites, Watch Later items, viewing history and selected preferences may be stored locally and, when you are signed in, synchronized with your account. Local data can be lost when browser storage or site data is cleared, so keep your own records for anything important.

5. Appropriate use

Use Vivid Cinema lawfully and respect the terms of the services it connects to. Do not attempt to bypass authentication, security controls, access restrictions or technical protections, or interfere with the service for other users.

6. Availability and information

Titles, ratings, artwork, release information, providers, trailers and features can change without notice. Vivid Cinema does not guarantee that a particular title, provider or playback source will always be available or that catalogue information will always be complete or current.

7. Changes to the service

Vivid Cinema may add, remove or change features as the service develops. These terms may also be updated. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.

8. Contact

For account, accessibility, content-display, privacy or security questions, use the Contact page. Never include a password, verification code or payment-card information in a support request.
